# Hologram Visual Direction

Parallax's public visual treatment is an original implementation of the
layered, kinetic holographic-strategy fantasy established by the reference
prototype. It must never use franchise logos, episode frames, actor likenesses,
or protected audio.

## On-board composition

- The playable field is a stack of transparent **vertical** grid panels, not a
  flat board. Plateaus recede through depth between the two player positions.
- Amber circuitry forms the persistent unowned topology; controlled tokens are
  vivid red or sensor blue, with shape, labels, and internal marks supporting
  color-independent readability.
- Nodes are luminous, flattened geometric discs. Links carry a slow travelling
  pulse, while sealed faces receive a restrained scanline shimmer.
- Each player's active cursor receives a curved blue energy arc from a tabletop
  sensor position. These arcs are presentation-only and never imply a rule or
  reveal hidden intent.
- Score treatment is large, monospaced, and floating at the top of the play
  space: player identity and rapidly changing digits should be readable before
  secondary telemetry.

## Quality guardrails

- Depth, occlusion, and camera movement must improve tactical readability;
  effects may not hide legal nodes, links, or outcomes.
- Keep emissive effects concentrated: the board should read as a dark room with
  sparse projected light, not as uniformly bright neon.
- Reduce Motion, high contrast, and color-vision-safe settings remain required.
  Never encode ownership or legality only through a red/blue distinction.
- The native Metal renderer owns holographic geometry. SwiftUI owns labels and
  accessibility semantics; game rules never depend on render coordinates.
